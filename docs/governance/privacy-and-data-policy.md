# Privacy and Data Policy

## Public portfolio rule
All examples are fabricated. Do not commit real station names/addresses, staff or customer names, phone numbers, supplier identities, bank details, balances, transactions, tank charts, photos, CCTV, credentials or raw operational logs.

Minimise data, use opaque synthetic IDs and redact personal/financial fields before model calls. A real deployment would require a documented lawful basis, retention schedule, access/deletion process, incident response and local legal review. Review model-provider retention, training-use, region, access and contractual terms before sending any real data.

## Publication checklist
- [ ] Search names, emails, phone numbers and addresses.
- [ ] Search bank/account identifiers and credentials.
- [ ] Confirm station/event IDs are synthetic.
- [ ] Confirm sample dip charts and values are fabricated.
- [ ] Remove raw source documents and screenshots.
- [ ] Review Git history for secrets before public release.
